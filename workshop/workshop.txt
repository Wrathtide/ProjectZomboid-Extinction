description=[h1]EXTINCTION[/h1]
description=[i]The dead will not walk forever.[/i]
description=
description=[b]Face a true apocalypse.[/b]
description=Humanity is on the brink of extinction. Become one of the few survivors who will rebuild the world from the ground up.
description=
description=The constant fear of zombie attacks gradually gives way to silence, isolation, and—with an NPC mod—conflict with the few survivors who remain. The catastrophe does not leave a clean, empty map. It leaves a world that remembers.
description=
description=[h2]NEW IN 1.1 — TWO PATHS TO EXTINCTION[/h2]
description=[b]THE ORIGINAL TIMELINE[/b]
description=Choose how many days the outbreak lasts. Each ordinary zombie receives a permanent random death date between apocalypse day 0 and your deadline: 21 days by default.
description=
description=[b]NATURAL EXTINCTION — OPTIONAL[/b]
description=Enable it when creating a new world. Zombies gradually exhaust a biological reserve, benefit from rain, and feed on existing human, zombie, or animal corpses. Food is finite, decomposition reduces it, and each feeding cycle loses part of the available resources.
description=
description=Some die early. Others survive longer by finding food. There is no guaranteed final day—and the possibility of a survivor keeps the silence uneasy.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Off by default: the original timeline remains available.
description=[*]Locks the fixed extinction deadline when selected.
description=[*]One biological-reserve value per zombie, capped at 21 baseline days even after feeding or rain.
description=[*]A simplified gameplay model, not a medical simulation.
description=[*]Zombies never attack other living zombies for food.
description=[*]Rotten flesh loses nutritional value, but is not toxic to zombies.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]THE HUNT CONTINUES[/h2]
description=[b]Zombies hunt living animals[/b] is a separate checkbox. It works with either extinction mode in new worlds: pursuit, bites, native animal death, and corpses. Natural mode also lets hungry zombies feed on those remains.
description=
description=[h2]THE LANDSCAPE REMEMBERS[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Silent ambient deaths: no player attack, kill credit, or combat XP.
description=[*]Historical death dates in regions explored long after the outbreak.
description=[*]Configurable age-based skeletonization: 180 days by default; 0 disables the age timer.
description=[*]In natural mode, fully consumed corpses become matching native skeletons, even if the age timer is disabled.
description=[*]New-world skeletonization preserves hard loot and bag contents while removing selected clothing and soft bags.
description=[*]Optional diagnostic overlays show zombie reserve, corpse resources, and time until age-based skeletonization.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]YOUR SANDBOX. YOUR POPULATION.[/h2]
description=Extinction does not replace the vanilla population system or rewrite its settings. Population, peak day, distribution, migration, respawn, start date, apocalypse age, and corpse sickness remain your choices.
description=
description=Starting six months after the apocalypse with a one-month fixed extinction deadline? Ordinary zombies are already past their death dates. Respawned zombies after that deadline become historically dated remains.
description=
description=Standard corpse-removal settings still matter: disable removal if you want lasting remains. Extinction specifically preserves native animal skeletons against later animal-rot deletion.
description=
description=[h2]UPDATING AN EXISTING WORLD[/h2]
description=[b]Existing fixed-timeline Extinction saves keep their original rules and death dates.[/b]
description=The update does not silently enable natural extinction or animal hunting in those worlds. Original skeleton-loot handling is retained too.
description=
description=[b]Start a new world to select natural extinction or the new hunting option.[/b]
description=Your choices are saved with that world. Natural-mode development saves already containing active sector records retain their existing mode.
description=
description=Keep a backup of important saves. Do not activate both a local test copy and the Workshop copy at the same time. Use this mod at your own risk.
description=
description=[h2]LET THE SURVIVORS TELL THEIR STORIES[/h2]
description=An NPC mod is strongly recommended for the late game. Compatibility protections are specifically implemented for [b]Project A-Life [ALIFE NPCS][/b]: marked NPC characters are not killed or skeletonized by Extinction. Unmarked ordinary zombies still follow extinction rules. Other NPC mods have not been verified.
description=
