title=Extinction
description=Extinction makes the ordinary zombie population die out gradually and permanently while preserving every standard Project Zomboid sandbox choice.
description=
description=Each ordinary zombie receives one immutable random death age between apocalypse day 0 and the configurable extinction deadline (21 days by default). The schedule uses the standard Months Since the Apocalypse setting: if a world starts six months after the outbreak and extinction was complete after one month, every ordinary zombie is already dead when the player begins.
description=
description=Zombies generated in newly explored areas receive historical death dates from the original outbreak period. Death is silent and gives no player kill credit. Old ordinary zombie corpses can become native lightweight skeletons after a configurable delay (180 days by default); 0 disables skeletonization.
description=
description=The mod never changes zombie population, peak population, distribution, respawn, corpse removal, corpse sickness, start date, or any other standard sandbox option. Zombie quantities are generated entirely by vanilla rules. If respawn is enabled, replacement zombies created after the deadline immediately become historically dated remains. If corpse removal is enabled, vanilla may remove remains normally.
description=
description=Compatible with Project A-Life: marked NPC shells are never killed or skeletonized. Unmarked real zombies from vanilla or other mods remain subject to extinction.
description=
description=Designed for new Build 42.20+ worlds. Full English documentation is included in README.md.
